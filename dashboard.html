What it does

  1. 01

    Whop Signal Monitor

    Captures and logs trade signals directly from Whop livestreams in real time, creating a clean timestamped record of every call without manual entry.

  2. 02

    Trades Dashboard

    Displays a structured list of trades taken so you can review instruments, entries, exits, and outcomes across sessions at a glance.

  3. 03

    Risk Lab Simulator

    Projects your logged trades with simulated gains based on trade size, generating estimated PnL so you can model position sizing before scaling contracts.

  4. 04

    One-Click Recap Studio

    Builds Daily, Weekly, and Monthly performance scorecards alongside a clean monospace social post preview ready to copy and share in one click.
