What it does

  1. 01

    Market Prep

    Establishes directional bias and marks key higher-timeframe futures levels before the open so every setup stays anchored to market structure.

  2. 02

    Setup Rules

    Defines repeatable price action setups and entry confirmation checklists to separate high-probability triggers from session noise.

  3. 03

    Risk & Sizing

    Specifies invalidation stops, position sizing rules, and reward-to-risk requirements so risk remains consistent across market conditions.

  4. 04

    Execution & Interactive Map

    Walks through live trade management from entry to exit and visualizes how every playbook rule connects inside an interactive map.
