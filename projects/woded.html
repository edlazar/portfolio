What it does

  1. 01

    Daily & Past Workouts

    Shows today's workout front and center along with an archive of past sessions so you can jump straight into training.

  2. 02

    Rx, Fx & Scaled Options

    Includes clear Rx, Fx, and Scaled variations for every workout so movements, reps, and loads fit your training level.

  3. 03

    Training Timers

    Runs built-in EMOM, AMRAP, and For Time timers directly alongside the workout prescription without switching apps.

  4. 04

    Workout Logging & History

    Records completed workouts, scores, and scaling tiers in a persistent training log to track consistency and progress over time.
